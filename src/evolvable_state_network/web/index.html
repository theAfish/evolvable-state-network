ASYNCHRONOUS EVOLUTION

Train local rules by how long they remain healthy.

One candidate life is one node-and-edge genome tested simultaneously on several graph replicas. It ends at a pathology death threshold or a curriculum survival milestone. Edge dynamics are learned, not damped: a never-active edge state, persistent gate saturation, or continued latent growth counts as a survival pathology. Finished lives update the elite archive and, in comparable batches, CMA-ES.
