ASYNCHRONOUS EVOLUTION

Train local rules by how long they remain healthy.

One candidate life is one CMA-ES-generated node-and-edge genome tested simultaneously on several graph replicas. A stage evaluates one comparable CMA cohort before updating its search distribution, then continues until a stable survivor population reaches its milestone. Edge dynamics are learned, not damped. Training stores health aggregates only; a replay is reconstructed only when you explicitly request debugging.
